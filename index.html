About Us

Supporting cooperative business growth with practical documentation guidance.

Documentation Centre works with individuals, entrepreneurs, farmers, traders, existing cooperative societies, and organisations who are exploring cooperative business opportunities in Bihar and Jharkhand.

Our focus is to help clients understand the essentials of cooperative business formation, relevant documentation, and the regulatory framework while supporting their business planning and expansion goals.

We support businesses and groups that want to build stronger collective ventures, improve documentation quality, and move forward with more confidence in their cooperative journey.
